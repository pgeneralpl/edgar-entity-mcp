Acceptable Use Policy

edgar-entity-mcp · Terms · Acceptable Use · Privacy · Contact & Abuse

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to everyone who uses the hosted edgar-entity-mcp service (the "Service"), directly or through an AI agent. It is part of our Terms of Service. You are responsible for what your agents, applications and end users do with the Service.

You must not

  1. Bypass limits or access controls. Do not circumvent rate limits, quotas, API-key requirements or blocks. This includes rotating IP addresses or keys, creating many accounts or keys, or spreading traffic across clients to get around limits.
  2. Bulk harvest or redistribute. Do not use the Service to systematically download, scrape or mirror large parts of the underlying datasets, or to build a competing dataset or service from the Service's output. If you need bulk data, get it directly from the SEC or the state open-data portals under their terms.
  3. Harass, stalk or dox people. Do not use the Service to locate, track, profile, harass, intimidate, stalk or expose individuals. This includes registered agents, officers, directors, insiders named in Form 4 filings, and owners of small businesses whose registered or business address may be their home. Do not compile personal profiles or publish personal contact or location data from the Service for these purposes.
  4. Make FCRA-type eligibility decisions. Do not use the Service or its output as a factor in decisions about a person's eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing or tenancy (including tenant screening), or for any other purpose regulated by the U.S. Fair Credit Reporting Act or similar laws.
  5. Automate abuse. Do not send excessive, looping or runaway automated requests, deliberately defeat caching, or run load or stress tests against the hosted endpoint. If you need high volume, self-host the open-source package.
  6. Attack the Service. Do not probe, scan or test the Service for vulnerabilities without following our security reporting process. Do not run denial-of-service attacks, inject malicious input, try to gain unauthorized access, or interfere with other users.
  7. Abuse upstream sources. Do not use the Service to violate, or to help anyone violate, the SEC's fair-access policy (including its request-rate limits and User-Agent requirements) or the terms of any state open-data portal or Socrata API. Do not use the Service to disguise the origin of traffic to those sources.
  8. Act unlawfully or deceptively. Do not use the Service for fraud, market manipulation, securities-law violations, impersonation, or to claim falsely that data is endorsed by the SEC or a state. Do not use the data in breach of privacy or data-protection law.
  9. Resell access. Do not resell, sublicense or provide paid access to the hosted Service without our written permission. The open-source code may be used under the MIT License.

Enforcement

Without notice, we may throttle, suspend or permanently block the users, API keys, IP addresses or networks involved, remove access to specific records, and report unlawful activity to the relevant authorities where appropriate or required by law. We decide on enforcement at our discretion, in proportion to the violation.
